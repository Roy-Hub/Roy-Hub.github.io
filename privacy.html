Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the personal software project "Hermes Workspace" ("the app"), a personal automation assistant operated by Mathew Roy ("the developer") for use solely on the developer's own Google account and personal devices.

Information we access

With the account owner's explicit OAuth authorization, the app can access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts data belonging to the account owner, in order to perform automation tasks the owner requests (reading and organizing email, managing calendar events, reading and creating documents and files).

How information is used and stored

All accessed data is processed locally on the owner's own hardware. Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's device with restricted file permissions. The app does not use Google data for advertising, does not human-review Gmail content, and does not create derived data sets.

Retention and revocation

The account owner may revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/connections and may delete any data the app created. Access tokens are refreshed only as needed to serve the owner's requests.
